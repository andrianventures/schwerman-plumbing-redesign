2. Replace illustrative stock with approved real project/team photography when provided. Approve any source-site/Facebook asset reuse.
3. Confirm the legitimate Google Business Profile; use rating/testimonials only with accurate source attribution and permission.
4. Approve a real privacy policy and accessibility process; the preview pages are draft explanations, not legal advice.
5. Set the final HTTPS base with `SCHWERMAN_BASE`, remove the preview `noindex` safeguard only after owner approval, check all links and structured data, and redirect old URLs where the owner controls them.
6. Decide whether enquiries should be visitor-sent drafts on GitHub Pages or automatically stored by the included Node server on a secure, Node-capable host; train the owner to check the private inbox and arrange backups/retention.
