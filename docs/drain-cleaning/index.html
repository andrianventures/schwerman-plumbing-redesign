DRAIN WORK

Drain Rodding & Cleaning in Lake County, IL

Schwerman explicitly lists drain rodding in its service menu. Tell the team whether one fixture or several are affected, when the problem began and whether you hear gurgling or notice an odor.

A recurring clog may have a different cause from a one-time buildup. Avoid assuming that a quick chemical treatment solves a line problem, especially if multiple drains are slow or there is a backup.

If wastewater is returning through a floor drain or toilet, treat it as more urgent and call directly. The appropriate repair depends on the line and what an inspection finds.
