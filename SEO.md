Every page has its own title, description, H1, readable prose, call link, breadcrumbs, and a visible question/answer with matching structured data. The schema uses `Plumber`, `Service` where appropriate, `WebPage`, `WebSite`, `BreadcrumbList` and `FAQPage`; it does **not** include unverifiable review stars or credentials. The public proposal has `noindex,nofollow` until owner approval and final-domain migration, so it should **not** compete with the company's current site.
